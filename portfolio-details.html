To-Do List Mobile Application

Design Process


Project Goals: The primary goal was to design a minimalistic yet feature-rich mobile app that allows users to manage their daily tasks. The app needed to be intuitive, allowing users to quickly add, edit, or complete tasks without unnecessary friction. A focus on user engagement was maintained through a rewarding UX and clean UI.
Target Audience: This app was designed for users across various age groups who need a simple and effective task manager. The clean interface and focus on ease of use make it accessible for both younger users and professionals.

This app addresses the user's need for effective task management by providing:
Intuitive Navigation: Simple tab navigation at the bottom of the screen allows users to switch between views (Home, Tasks, Calendar, and Profile) effortlessly.
Task Creation: A streamlined interface for creating and managing tasks makes staying organized easier than ever.
Personalization: Users can customize their profile information and receive task completion rewards, adding a personal touch to their productivity journey.
Calendar Integration:Calendar Integration: The calendar feature helps users keep track of tasks in the broader context of their monthly schedule, improving planning.
